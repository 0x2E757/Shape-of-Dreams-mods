Picking up an essence you already have no longer merges it into the one you have. It goes to your hand, and you choose a slot for it the way you would for any new essence - so the same essence can be worn in several memories, or twice in one.

[b]Strongly recommended alongside it: Are My Gems Compatible.[/b] It marks an essence that can never fire in the memory it is in - and with it loaded, such a copy (a second Essence of Lava in a memory that deals no Fire damage, say) does not count and weakens none of the copies that work.
[url]https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3794045595[/url]

[h2]Merging, when you want it[/h2]
With the new essence in hand, choose the slot of the one you already have and the two merge, as picking it up used to do. Its tooltip says so before you click, with the quality it will end up at. Choose an empty slot instead and you keep both. Two copies you already wear merge by dragging one onto the other in the edit screen.

[h2]Every copy is weaker[/h2]
Every value of the essence that grows with its quality is cut, once per effect, and nothing else - a duration, a threshold or a cooldown that stays the same at every quality stays the same here.
[list]
[*]Equipped in two memories: every copy -30%. In three or more: -40%.
[*]Two copies in one memory: those copies -25%. Three or more: -35%.
[/list]
The two multiply: two copies in Q and one in W leave each copy in Q at 52.5% and the one in W at 70%. All four numbers are settings.

The essence's tooltip shows the numbers it really has where it is - or where you are dragging it - and a line under them says how much it has been weakened and why.

[h2]Copies add up[/h2]
Two copies do what two essences would. Where the game keeps one thing per hero - a buff on you, a mark on an enemy, a cooldown per enemy - each copy keeps its own, and taking one copy off takes only what is its own.

[h2]In co-op[/h2]
[b]The host decides.[/b] Picking up, equipping and every number an essence produces are the host's, so a party plays by the host's copy of the mod and its settings. Merging - by choosing a slot or by dragging - works for everyone in the party, guests without the mod included. A guest with the mod sees the host's settings in its tooltips, not its own.
