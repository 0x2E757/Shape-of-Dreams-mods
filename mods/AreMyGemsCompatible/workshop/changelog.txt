[h2]1.3[/h2]
[list]
[*]Essences beside it now count for what they do themselves when they fire: Essence of Guidance beside Essence of Blossoming or Essence of Clemency, which heal through the memory, is no longer marked, nor is Essence of Love beside those or beside Essence of Rigidity, which gives a barrier
[*]Essence of Sulfur and Essence of the Abyss turn all of the memory's damage to Fire and to Dark, and are now taken as doing so: an essence waiting for Cold damage beside them is marked even in a memory that deals Cold damage, and Essence of Fever beside Essence of the Abyss even in one that deals Fire damage
[*]Essences that empower the next cast now need that cast to deal damage, not only to happen: Essence of Talc, Essence of Shatter, Essence of Finality, Essence of Responsibility, Pure White, Essence of the Celestial, Heart of Gold, Essence of Overload and Essence of Rejuvenation are marked in a memory that deals none
[*]Fixed: an essence waiting for damage was never marked in Hero's Return - the words "Attack Damage" in its description were taken for the memory dealing damage
[*]Fixed: Essence of Frost beside Essence of Sharpness was never marked, though the arrows deal no Cold damage
[/list]

[h2]1.2[/h2]
[list]
[*]Essences that answer to one element are now checked: Essence of Frost and Essence of Apathy wait for Cold damage, Essence of Fever and Essence of Lava for Fire damage, and each is marked in a memory that never deals it - Essence of Frost in Incendiary Rounds is the case that was reported
[*]An essence beside it that changes the element of the memory's damage is taken into account: Essence of Inversion can turn Fire into Cold, so Essence of Frost beside it is not marked; Essence of Sulfur only ever makes Fire, so it still is
[*]Essence of Frost used to go unmarked everywhere, even in a memory that deals no damage at all
[/list]

[h2]1.1[/h2]
[list]
[*]Essences in identity memories are now checked too, for when a shrine gives one an essence slot - an identity memory is never cast, so an essence that works off the cast is marked there
[*]An identity memory is judged by what it actually does rather than by its description, so one that only raises your Attack Damage is no longer taken for a memory that deals damage
[*]Essences in Movement memories are now checked too, for mods that give those an essence slot
[/list]
