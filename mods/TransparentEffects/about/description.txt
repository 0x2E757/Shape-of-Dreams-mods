Skill effects can be made see-through - your own and other players', set separately.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Opacity of my own effects[/b] - 1 is the game untouched, 0 is invisible
[*][b]Opacity of other players' effects[/b]
[/list]

Only how effects are drawn changes. Nothing moves, hits or lands differently, and enemies are never touched.

The game's own "reduce other players' effects" setting is still there and still applies on top of the second row.

[h2]In co-op[/h2]
Nobody else needs it and nobody else sees anything different - each client decides how to draw the effects on its own screen.
